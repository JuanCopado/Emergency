# v1.36 ICU sedation/analgesia consolidation — 2026-10-02

## Sources rechecked
- SCCM PADIS 2018.
- SCCM PADIS focused update 2025.
- Current dexmedetomidine product information.

## Findings
- Analgesia-first and light-sedation strategy remains consistent with PADIS.
- PADIS 2018 preference for propofol or dexmedetomidine over benzodiazepines remains appropriate.
- PADIS 2025 specifically suggests dexmedetomidine over propofol when light sedation and/or delirium reduction are highest priorities; this is not a universal superiority statement.
- Dexmedetomidine ICU loading dose remains not recommended; maximum 1.4 micrograms/kg/h in the referenced European product information.
- Existing mL/h arithmetic regression tests remain in place.
- Portugal/Azores product strengths, local concentrations, compatibility/stability and formulary rules remain localization QA gates.
