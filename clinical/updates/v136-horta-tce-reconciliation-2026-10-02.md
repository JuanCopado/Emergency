## Local pathway captured
- ABCDE + stabilization.
- Avoid SpO2 <94% / PaO2 <60 mmHg and SBP <90 mmHg.
- GCS <=8: airway/sedation/ventilation pathway when appropriate; monitor for intracranial hypertension.
- GCS 15 asymptomatic/no RF: discharge with competent adult observation >=24 h and alarm instructions.
- GCS 15 symptomatic/no RF: CT; if normal, >=6 h ED observation.
- GCS 15 + >=1 local risk factor or GCS 13--14: CT.
- Anticoagulated GCS 15 with normal CT: local >=12 h ED observation exception.
- GCS 9--12: CT + neurosurgical contact.
- GCS <=8: CT + neurosurgical contact; cervical-spine CT if unconscious or cervical injury suspected.
- Immediate repeat CT for symptomatic deterioration, >=2 GCS decline, intracranial-hypertension signs or new focal deficit.
