# v1.36 respiratory-support consolidation — 2026-10-02

## Sources
- ERS clinical practice guideline: HFNC in acute respiratory failure (2022).
- Official ERS/ATS NIV guideline for acute respiratory failure (2017).

## Findings
- HFNO remains green and already aligns well with ERS 2022.
- Acute respiratory failure now explicitly separates oxygenation from ventilation failure.
- In de-novo acute hypoxemic respiratory failure, HFNO is preferred over NIV when no immediate intubation indication exists.
- NIV remains appropriate for COPD with acute respiratory acidosis and acute cardiogenic pulmonary edema.
- NIV/HFNO must not delay intubation when the clinical trajectory worsens.

## Status
acute-respiratory-failure and noninvasive-ventilation remain YELLOW pending human respiratory/ICU and local device/protocol review.
