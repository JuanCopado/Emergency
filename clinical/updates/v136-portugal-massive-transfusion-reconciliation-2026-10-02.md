## Important reconciliation findings
- The user-supplied 2014 PDF is not the latest DGS version.
- The historical DGS fixed pack 4 CE + 4 PFC + 1 CPP is retained as local historical context, not a universal current standard.
- For traumatic bleeding, the current TXA regimen is 1 g IV over 10 min + 1 g over 8 h within 3 h of injury; inconsistent older DGS wording is not propagated.
- Fibrinogen treatment threshold remains <=1.5 g/L, but current European initial supplementation is 3--4 g or cryoprecipitate equivalent, guided thereafter by VEM/laboratory results.
- Ionised calcium should be monitored and maintained in the normal range; calcium chloride is preferred for major-trauma hypocalcaemia in the European guideline.
- rFVIIa is not first-line; it is rescue-only after conventional haemostatic and physiologic correction measures fail.
- Agent-specific anticoagulant reversal supersedes a generic PCC-by-ROTEM approach.
