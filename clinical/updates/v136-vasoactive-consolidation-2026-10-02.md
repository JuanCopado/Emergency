# v1.36 vasoactive/inotrope consolidation — 2026-10-02

## Sources rechecked
- Surviving Sepsis Campaign adult guideline 2026 (SCCM).
- Current U.S. norepinephrine and vasopressin prescribing information for concentration/label semantics.

## Findings
- Adult septic-shock sequence remains consistent: norepinephrine first line; add vasopressin with escalating norepinephrine; add epinephrine if MAP remains inadequate.
- Vasopressin label range 0.01--0.07 U/min and dilution to 0.1 or 1 U/mL reconfirmed.
- Norepinephrine safety correction: do not infer a fixed molecular conversion from a product name containing bitartrate/tartrate. Use the product's declared norepinephrine-equivalent content. This matters because current products may state strength as norepinephrine equivalent while the active substance is bitartrate.
- Portugal/Azores exact presentation, RCM, standard concentration, stability and line policy remain unverified and are therefore a QA localization gate.
