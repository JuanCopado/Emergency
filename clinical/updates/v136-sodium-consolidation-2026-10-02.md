# v1.36 sodium-emergency consolidation — 2026-10-02

## Findings
The existing sodium module was already largely concordant with European guidance. The consolidation therefore avoided unnecessary rewriting.

## Changes
- Preserved 150 mL 3% saline over 20 min for symptomatic hyponatraemia.
- Preserved approximately 5 mmol/L initial target rise.
- Strengthened 24-h correction ceilings and high-risk osmotic-demeylination caution.
- Made relowering and water-diuresis monitoring explicit.
- Preserved serum/urine diagnostic classification and electrolyte-free water clearance.

## Status
YELLOW: authoritative European hyponatraemia guidance is older and local hypertonic-saline/monitoring standards remain to be reviewed.
