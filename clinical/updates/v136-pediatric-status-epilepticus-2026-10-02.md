# v1.36 pediatric status epilepticus consolidation — 2026-10-02

## Source
HSE National Clinical Guideline CDI/0277/1.0/2025, Version 0, effective 15/10/2025, revision due 15/10/2028.

## Added/confirmed
- Hypoglycemia <2.6 mmol/L: 10% glucose 3 mL/kg, recheck at 10 min.
- Maximum two total benzodiazepine doses including prehospital doses.
- Buccal midazolam 0.3 mg/kg; IV lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg max 4 mg; rectal diazepam 0.5 mg/kg max 20 mg.
- Levetiracetam 40 mg/kg max 3 g, dilute to 50 mg/mL, infuse over 5 min.
- Phenytoin 20 mg/kg max 2 g then phenobarbital 20 mg/kg max 1 g in the HSE sequence.
- Consultant alternatives: lacosamide 10 mg/kg and sodium valproate 20 mg/kg IV max 3 g.
- Explicit exclusion of neonates <1 month and breakthrough seizures above baseline in established epilepsy.

YELLOW pending local pediatric neurology/pharmacy, formulary, EEG and transfer-pathway verification.
