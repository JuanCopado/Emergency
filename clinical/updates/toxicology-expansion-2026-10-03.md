# Toxicology expansion — 2026-10-03

Branch: `v1.36-consolidation`.

## Scope
Expanded existing IDs without creating duplicates:
- `toxicology`
- `anticoagulation-reversal`

Coverage now explicitly includes recreational/sympathomimetic drugs (cocaine, amphetamines, methamphetamine, MDMA, synthetic cathinones, opioids, GHB, cannabinoids, PCP/ketamine/LSD), serotonergic and anticholinergic syndromes; paracetamol, salicylates, sodium-channel blockers, bupropion, lithium, valproate, isoniazid, sulfonylureas, insulin, metformin, methotrexate, colchicine, digoxin; beta-blockers/CCB; cyanide, CO, methemoglobinemia, hydrogen sulfide; methanol/ethylene glycol/isopropanol; organophosphates/carbamates, anticoagulant rodenticides, paraquat/diquat, strychnine, amatoxin, marine toxins, snake/arthropod envenomation, botulism; iron, lead, arsenic/mercury, thallium, hydrofluoric acid, caustics and hydrocarbons.

Anticoagulant reversal now separates VKA/superwarfarin, dabigatran, apixaban/rivaroxaban, edoxaban, UFH, LMWH, fondaparinux, argatroban, bivalirudin, danaparoid, thrombolytics and antiplatelet agents, explicitly identifying specific, partial, nonspecific and absent antidotes.

## Principal evidence reviewed
- AHA 2023 Focused Update on cardiac arrest/life-threatening poisoning.
- 2023 US/Canada acetaminophen consensus.
- EXTRIP recommendations for salicylate, lithium, metformin, methanol, ethylene glycol and selected toxins.
- ASRA LAST checklist (current version reviewed; update expected 2027).
- EMA Praxbind product information (updated 2026) and EMA Ondexxya product information (2025).
- AHA/ASA 2022 spontaneous ICH anticoagulant reversal guidance.
- ACC anticoagulant-related bleeding pathway and ISTH SSC DOAC reversal guidance.

## Release gate
Status remains **yellow**. This expansion is a high-risk clinical reference and requires human toxicology/hematology/pharmacy review plus Portugal/Azores verification of CIAV pathway, INFARMED/formulary, antidote stock, PCC brand-specific dosing, concentrations and dialysis/ECMO availability before clinical release.
