# Evidence and update policy

## Source hierarchy
1. Current international/national guideline
2. High-quality systematic review/meta-analysis
3. Randomized trial
4. Large observational evidence
5. Expert consensus / local protocol

## Update-sensitive topics
Always verify current guidance before definitive protocol-level advice for:
- stroke reperfusion
- ACS antithrombotic strategy
- PE thrombolysis/reperfusion
- sepsis bundles/vasopressors
- cardiac arrest algorithms
- difficult airway
- pediatric resuscitation
- pregnancy emergencies
- antidotes
- anticoagulant reversal
- antibiotic choice
- procedural sedation

State guideline year when relevant.
