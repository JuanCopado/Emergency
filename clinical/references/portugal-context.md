# Portugal clinical and medication context

Use for care in Portugal, including the Azores, or when the user requests Portuguese alignment. Preserve the user's response language; use Portuguese generic/product terminology where it prevents confusion.

## Sources to check
- DGS clinical norms: https://normas.dgs.min-saude.pt/category/normas-clinicas/
- DGS alternate official index: https://www.dgs.pt/normas-orientacoes-e-informacoes/normas-e-circulares-normativas.aspx
- INFARMED medication search/Infomed: https://www.infarmed.pt/web/infarmed/servicos-on-line/pesquisa-do-medicamento
- INFARMED safety and availability information: https://www.infarmed.pt/
- Relevant regional health authority and receiving hospital protocols for referral, transfer, local formulary and antimicrobial resistance. Do not assume mainland pathways or resources are identical in the Azores.

## Reconciliation workflow
1. Identify the relevant DGS Norma or Orientação and inspect its actual text. Record number, title, original date, revision date, scope, source URL and any supersession/revocation notice. Do not treat an old indexed PDF as proof of current status.
2. Compare it with current specialty guidance and the local service protocol. Explain clinically material differences, evidence dates and applicability; do not silently replace either source or assume a document is legally mandatory from its title alone.
3. For drugs, verify the exact Portuguese product RCM, strength, route, dilution, compatibility, stability and safety alerts. Distinguish authorization, marketing/supply status and actual hospital stock. Do not infer availability of nicardipine, clevidipine, urapidil or any alternative from foreign labels.
4. Give an appropriate available alternative when confirmed; otherwise label availability unknown. Continue to show dose, final volume (100/200 mL when compatible), concentration, mL/h, titration and monitoring. Never invent a locally approved concentration.
5. If Portuguese sources are inaccessible, state that limitation and use identified international evidence provisionally. Do not label the result DGS-compliant, nationally validated or locally adopted without verification. Do not delay immediate stabilization for document retrieval.
6. Place unresolved recommendation conflicts in the existing pending-update queue, linked to the affected module. Source access failure is not a completed guideline audit.
