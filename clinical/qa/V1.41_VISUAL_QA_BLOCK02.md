## QA rules

- One procedure = one image.
- Compare every image against the canonical procedure file before acceptance.
- Reject invented doses, universal parameters, lengths, gauges, depths, pressures, energy values, volumes, or unsupported contraindications.
- Reject wrong anatomy, wrong target vessel/site, wrong procedure ID, or cross-contamination from a previous image.
- Require essential anatomy, main maneuver, STOP points, and confirmation.
- Thoracic trauma images may be schematic/non-graphic when needed.
- Do not promote any procedure to GREEN from visual QA alone.
