## Current clinical-note capabilities
- Hybrid structured + free-text history.
- Serial timeline and repeated observations.
- Provenance-aware MCDT integration.
- Separate official report vs AI interpretation.
- Privacy preflight and pseudonymous encounter ID.
- DOCX/PDF/JSON export gated by privacy + clinician review.
- Diagnostic support output:
  - problem representation;
  - active problems;
  - likely diagnoses;
  - differential;
  - must-not-miss;
  - discriminating tests;
  - treatment/module routing;
  - disposition;
  - reassessment;
  - contradictions;
  - limitations.

## Diagnostic engine
- **92 executable syndrome rules**.
- **60 synthetic diagnostic scenarios PASS**.
- Qualitative confidence only: high / moderate / low.
- No fabricated calibrated probabilities.
- Short acronyms use token-boundary matching to avoid substring false positives.
- Adult/pediatric applicability can be age-gated.
- Explicit pediatric routing exists for cardiac arrest, arrhythmias, CNS infection, trauma, burns, toxicology, adrenal crisis, hypertensive emergency, haematology/oncology and other paediatric acute syndromes.
- Generated assessment resets clinician sign-off; clinician review is required again before export.
