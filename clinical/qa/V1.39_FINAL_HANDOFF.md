## Privacy
- Pseudonymous encounter UUID.
- Normal workflow does not require name, exact DOB, address, phone/email, SNS/NIF/ID/MRN.
- Pseudonymization is not treated as anonymization.
- Free-text privacy scanner detects labelled identifiers.
- Word-boundary fixes prevent false positives from clinical acronyms/words.
- External anonymized export requires stronger metadata/image checks.
- Burned-in identifiers must be checked for image-derived external export.
- Original clinical attachments are not embedded by default.
