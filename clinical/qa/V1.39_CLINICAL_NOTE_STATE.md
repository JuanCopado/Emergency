## Privacy
- Does not require patient name, exact DOB, address, telephone/email, SNS/NIF/ID/MRN.
- Uses pseudonymous encounter UUID.
- Pseudonymization is not described as anonymization.
- Free-text identifier scanner blocks export on direct-identifier patterns.
- External anonymized export requires additional metadata/privacy checks.
- Original uploaded clinical files are not embedded into exports by default.
- Image-derived content requires burned-in-identifier checking before external export.
- Generated DOCX/PDF metadata are generic.

## Diagnostic-support safety
- No unsupported calibrated numeric probabilities.
- Confidence: high / moderate / low.
- Preserve evidence for/against and missing discriminating data.
- Route disease management through existing modules rather than duplicate treatment logic.
- Stabilization/red flags before documentation completion.
- Clinician acceptance/edit required before export.
